# Cragward Marketing Plan: the call-option playbook

*Written 2026-07-03. Companion to `docs/growth-architecture-plan.md` (strategy) and
`docs/launch-kit.md` (the first post, drafts, checklist). This plan matches the agreed
frame: Cragward is a cheap call option. Spend hours not dollars, escalate only when
the tripwires fire, and optimize for a community flywheel, not reach. Budget: ~$0.
Time: fits inside Cole's ~3–5 hrs/week alongside actually climbing.*

---

## 0. Positioning (never drift from this)

**One-liner:** *Cragward gets you to the wall and works with no signal. Parking,
approach trails, and local beta for the crags you climb.*

- **Complement, not competitor.** Never trash Mountain Project; never accept the
  "MP killer" framing when others offer it. "MP tells you what to climb; Cragward
  gets you there and works offline." Complements get adopted; challengers get defended
  against. By incumbents *and* by users' loyalties.
- **Community-built, locally owned.** The product IS the contributed beta. All
  marketing is really contributor recruitment.
- **Voice:** a local climber sharing a tool he built, forever. Never startup-speak,
  never growth-hacky. The climbing community smells marketing instantly and punishes it.
- **Access-respectful, always.** Lead with the sensitive-access flag when the topic
  comes up. One access controversy could kill community trust permanently. This is
  the brand's biggest single risk.

## 1. The engine (what all activity feeds)

```
seeded beta → uniquely useful at YOUR crags → climbers share links / show partners
     ↑                                                        ↓
     └──── new users add ticks, pins, photos, corrections ←──┘
```

Marketing's only job is to inject people into this loop **in regions where the beta
is already good**. Never market a region before it's seeded, an empty app gets no
second visit, and you only get one first impression per community.

## 2. Phases: gated by the four tripwires, not by the calendar

**The tripwires** (from the strategy discussion; measure via Umami + Supabase):
T1 strangers retain (D30) · T2 strangers contribute unprompted · T3 organic requests
for new regions · T4 someone at a crag shows YOU the app.

### Phase A: Beachhead (now → first ~100 users). Cost: $0. Hours: 2–3/wk.
*Goal: prove T1–T2 in Grand Junction. Depth over reach.*

1. **Seed first** (launch-kit §0): parking + approach + photos on the 3–5 flagship
   crags. This is marketing, the product demo IS the data.
2. **The physical world beats the internet at this scale:**
   - QR-code cards (the launch-kit one-liner + cragward.com) at GJ gym boards, gear
     shops, trailhead kiosks where legal. Cheap to print, zero spam-smell.
   - **Crag evangelism**: show the app to whoever you share a wall with; offer to add
     THEIR crag's parking while standing there. Ten in-person converts who contribute
     beat a thousand impressions.
3. **One community post per venue, story-led** (drafts in launch-kit §1): GJ/Western
   Slope Facebook groups first, r/COclimbing second, MP's Colorado forum last and most
   carefully. Reply to every comment fast for the first two hours.
4. **Welcome every contributor personally**: a note reply, a thanks in the thread.
   Early contributors are gold; there will be ~5 and they become the co-marketers.
5. **Fix-and-report cadence:** post updates into the SAME thread ("added X, fixed Y,
   thanks @whoever"). A responsive builder is the cheapest retention and referral
   engine there is.

### Phase B: Region-by-region expansion (after T1+T2 fire). Hours: 3–5/wk.
*Goal: repeat the beachhead playbook where pull exists. Trigger T3.*

- Expand ONLY where a real evangelist will seed (the Alaska-friends rule). Each new
  region = targeted OpenBeta import + a local contributor + that region's own
  community post, written with/by the local.
- **Deputize locals**: give the most active contributor in each region visible status
  (attribution is built; consider "top contributor" recognition in-app later). People
  market what they feel ownership of.
- **Shareable moments over ads**: every route/wall link is the growth surface. When
  per-route OG previews become worth building (lots of shares in Umami), that's the
  signal to prerender them.
- Seasonal rhythm: post before each region's season starts (desert = spring/fall,
  alpine = summer). A "download before the weekend" reminder post is genuinely useful
  content, not spam.

### Phase C: Compounding (after T3, maybe T4). Only phase with real spend (<$500/yr).
*Goal: become the default second app across the Mountain West.*

- **Partnerships, not ads**: gyms (QR poster + "log your outdoor sends"), guide
  services and university outdoor programs (offline packs for clients/trips), local
  climbing coalitions/access funds (offer the app as a tool; their endorsement is the
  most trusted marketing that exists in climbing, and aligns with the access story).
- **Earned media**: pitch the story ("GJ climber built an offline approach app,
  community-run") to regional outlets and climbing newsletters/podcasts. The
  founder-story is the pitch; you have a good one.
- Revisit monetization seams (freemium offline / B2B sponsorship) only here, per the
  growth plan, and grandfather early contributors free, loudly. They built it.

## 3. Channel rules of thumb

| Channel | Use for | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| In person at crags/gyms | Phase A core | Highest conversion; always offer to add their beta on the spot |
| Local FB groups | Launch + updates | Story voice; same-thread updates; never cross-post spam |
| Reddit (r/COclimbing etc.) | One launch post per sub | Disclose "I built this" first line; read sub rules; mods are allies |
| MP forums | Carefully, later | Complement framing ONLY; expect skepticism; never argue |
| Instagram | Optional, Phase B+ | Screenshot-of-the-week with a real approach tip; low priority |
| QR cards/posters | Phase A/B | The one-liner + domain, nothing else |
| Paid ads | n/a | **Never in this plan.** Wrong economics for the TAM; poisons the community voice |
| SEO/content | n/a | Deferred indefinitely (MP owns it; growth plan §6) |

## 4. Metrics that matter (and the ones that don't)

Watch weekly in Umami + Supabase (5 min): visitors → sign_ups → **area_downloaded**
(the hero metric) → contribution_created (the flywheel metric) → shares. Plus the four
tripwires. Ignore: pageviews, follower counts, anything that feels good but doesn't
map to retain/contribute/pull.

**Kill-switch honesty:** if two seasons of Phase A produce no T1/T2, stop marketing.
Cragward is the excellent private tool it was originally scoped to be, and that's a
stated win. Don't grind against silence.
